# MoveABox @ CHRIST Central Campus Startup Fair
## Product-Market Fit Analysis & 30-Day Stall Playbook

**Prepared:** 18 August 2026
**Venue:** CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Central Campus — Hosur Road, S.G. Palya, Bhavani Nagar, Bengaluru 560029
**Owning branch:** MoveABox Koramangala (~2–3 km from campus gate)
**Window:** Stall-ready within 30 days (by ~18 September 2026)

---

## 1. Why this campus is a real market, not a branding exercise

CHRIST Central Campus carries **20,000+ students**; the university as a whole enrols
**22,793 students drawn from every state and union territory of India and 58 other
countries**. That national spread is the entire commercial thesis: a campus of local
students is a bad market for a mover, a campus of outstation students is an excellent one.

Geographically this is not a new market either. S.G. Palya sits inside the existing
**MoveABox Koramangala** catchment — Koramangala, BTM Layout, Madiwala, Ejipura, Adugodi,
Domlur, HSR. The PG belt the students live in (S.G. Palya, Tavarekere, Bhavani Nagar,
Ejipura, BTM) is already our service area. Every stall sale is fulfilled by a branch that
is a ten-minute drive away. **Marginal cost of serving this campus is close to zero.**

## 2. The four demand triggers — what actually makes a student buy from a mover

### Trigger 1 — The two-day vacate rule (the strongest hook we have)
CHRIST hostel accommodation is granted for one academic year, and **boarders must vacate
within two working days of their final examination**, taking all personal articles with
them. Every hosteller hits a hard deadline with a room full of accumulated stuff, in exam
week, with no vehicle and no plan. This is the single most relatable pain on campus, and
it is *calendared* — we know exactly when it fires.

### Trigger 2 — The 15 kg airline wall (the sharpest number in the pitch)
IndiGo's domestic check-in allowance is **15 kg**. Excess baggage runs **₹600/kg at the
airport counter**, or ₹350–450/kg if pre-booked online. A student going home after a
semester is routinely 20–30 kg over — books, winter clothes, quilt, induction cooktop,
sports gear.

Against that, our real landed cost (live Delhivery surface rates, origin 560029, pulled
from the MoveABox ERP on 18 Aug 2026):

| Destination | Weight | Our all-in cost | Effective ₹/kg |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delhi (110001) | 15 kg | ₹594.80 | ₹39.65 |
| Delhi (110001) | 25 kg | ₹961.88 | ₹38.48 |
| Kochi (682001) | 15 kg | ₹666.85 | ₹44.46 |
| Guwahati (781001) | 15 kg | ₹770.85 | ₹51.39 |
| Bengaluru local (560034) | 15 kg | ₹423.48 | ₹28.23 |

**20 kg of excess baggage costs ₹12,000 at the airport counter. The same 20 kg goes
door-to-door with us for under ₹900.** That is a 13x saving, it is true, and it is
verifiable on the spot. This one comparison should be the banner behind the stall.

Note the local rate: single-box intra-Bengaluru courier is *expensive* per kg relative to
a mini-truck. Intra-city is a **vehicle** business for us, not a parcel business. Price
accordingly.

### Trigger 3 — PG churn
Students around Central Campus switch PGs every semester or year. Nobody owns a vehicle;
autos refuse a mattress plus suitcase plus cycle; a professional local shift quotes
₹3,000–5,000, which is well beyond a student. A bare mini-truck starts at ~₹205–330 base
fare but leaves the student carrying everything up three floors alone. **The gap between
"too expensive" and "no help at all" is where our student SKU lives.**

### Trigger 4 — Nowhere to leave anything
There is no vacation storage at the hostel. Today the student's only two options are carry
it home (pay excess baggage) or throw it away. Existing Bengaluru student storage sells at
**₹500+/month per package, or ₹1,000–1,500 per bag per month**. We own warehouse space.
This is the highest-margin line in the portfolio and it is currently unserved on campus.

## 3. The stall constraint that shapes every SKU

A stall cannot fulfil. So every product must be one of three shapes:

- **(A) Carry-away physical goods** — instant cash, zero fulfilment risk, and the student
  walks across campus holding a MoveABox-branded box.
- **(B) Prepaid voucher redeemed later** — cash today, capacity consumed in the Oct–Nov
  wave, and it locks the student out of Porter and the S.G. Palya courier shops.
- **(C) Token booking** — a small ₹99–₹149 deposit against a larger job. Converts far
  better than a free sign-up sheet, because paying anything at all creates commitment.

**The volume band is ₹99–₹799.** That is what a student pays by UPI without calling a
parent. Above roughly ₹1,500 a parent enters the decision and conversion collapses. Build
the volume at ₹99–₹799 and let two or three high-ticket SKUs ride on top of it.

## 4. The product list — 13 matches, ranked

### TIER 1 — These carry the stall

**1. MB-KIT-02 · The Vacate Kit — ₹349** *(carry-away)*
2 large cartons + 1 small + 20 m bubble wrap + 2 tapes + marker + fragile stickers.
Companion SKU: **MB-KIT-01 Mini Kit — ₹149** (1 carton, tape, marker).
Landed cost ~₹150–180 per kit against Bangalore wholesale packing rates; retail comparison
is CartonBoxWala's ₹3,499 home combo, so ₹349 reads as obviously cheap.
- *Relatability:* every hosteller knows the pre-vacate scramble for boxes.
- *Volume:* **VERY HIGH** — pure impulse, no fulfilment risk, highest expected unit count.
- *Strategic value:* branded boxes stacked in hostel corridors for the next two months are
  the cheapest advertising we will ever buy on this campus.
- *30-day lift:* LOW. Order 600 cartons from a Bengaluru supplier (5–7 day lead time).

**2. MB-BOX-15 · Campus-to-Home Box — ₹999 flat, all-India, 15 kg** *(prepaid voucher)*
Includes carton, packing, pickup from hostel gate or PG, door delivery anywhere in India by
surface, WhatsApp tracking. Upsell: **MB-BOX-30 at ₹1,799 for 30 kg.**
Margin ranges ₹160 (Guwahati) to ₹330 (Delhi) on the 15 kg SKU; the 30 kg SKU is materially
better and is what students actually need — push it.
- *Relatability:* this is the airline-excess-baggage rescue. It is the hero pitch.
- *Volume:* **HIGH.** This is the revenue anchor.
- *30-day lift:* LOW. Delhivery rate API is already live in our ERP.
- *Pricing note:* keep it flat nationally. Zone-based pricing halves stall conversion — the
  student cannot compute it in ten seconds, so they walk.

**3. MB-DOC-01 · Document Courier — ₹99 flat, all-India, tracked** *(carry-away service)*
Transcripts, mark sheets, bonafide certificates, visa files, internship offer letters, in a
stiff tamper-proof sleeve.
- *Relatability:* CHRIST students courier documents constantly — GRE/CAT, visa applications,
  apostille, joining letters — and the local courier shops overcharge and lose things.
- *Volume:* **VERY HIGH count, thin margin (~₹40).**
- *Its real job is not margin.* It is the lowest-friction first transaction on the campus:
  it converts a stranger into a customer with our number saved in their phone. Treat the
  margin as an acquisition cost that happens to be positive.

**4. MB-DESK-01 · Campus Parcel Desk — ₹399 per semester** *(recurring)*
Use the MoveABox Koramangala branch as your Bengaluru delivery address. Amazon, Flipkart,
Myntra orders that hostels refuse or that vanish from the common room. WhatsApp alert on
arrival, 7-day free hold, collect any time.
- *Relatability:* **highest daily-life relevance of anything on this list.** Hostel parcel
  chaos is universal and constant, not seasonal.
- *Volume:* **VERY HIGH sign-up rate.** Near-zero marginal cost — the branch is already staffed.
- *Strategic value:* this is the one SKU that converts MoveABox from a once-a-year service
  into a weekly habit. Every collection visit is an upsell conversation.
- *30-day lift:* MEDIUM. Needs a labelled shelf, an intake register, and a WhatsApp workflow.

**5. MB-POOL-01 · Group Box / Floor Pool — ₹599 per person at 4+ to the same region**
Four students shipping to Kerala share one consignment. Or five boxes from one hostel floor
booked into a single pickup slot.
- *Relatability:* CHRIST students move in regional cohorts — the Kerala, North-East, Delhi
  and Bihar groups are real, tight, and already on WhatsApp.
- *Volume:* **the multiplier on SKU 2.** One sign-up brings four. Give the organiser their
  own box free at five conversions and the student becomes an unpaid salesperson.
- *Ops:* pooled pickups collapse our per-box handling cost, so the discount partly pays for
  itself. This is the single most important idea for hitting *larger volume*.

### TIER 2 — High margin, sold as tokens

**6. MB-STOR-01 · Vacation Storage — ₹299 per box per month** *(₹99 token at the stall)*
Minimum 2 months, free pickup from hostel or PG, free redelivery when they return.
Undercuts the ₹500–1,500/month market rates decisively.
- *Relatability:* "Leave it, don't lug it." Aimed at the Dasara/Diwali break and semester end.
- *Volume:* **HIGH in Oct–Nov.** Warehouse-efficient — uniform boxes stack cleanly.
- *Mechanic:* sell a ₹99 rate-lock token, not the full amount. Balance on pickup.

**7. MB-SHIFT-01 · PG-to-PG Shift — ₹1,299 flat within 5 km** *(₹149 token)*
One Tata Ace, two porters, load and unload, up to 15 items. Covers S.G. Palya, Tavarekere,
Bhavani Nagar, Ejipura, BTM, Koramangala, Madiwala.
- *Relatability:* everyone has done the three-autos-and-a-broken-suitcase move.
- *Volume:* MEDIUM–HIGH count at a strong ticket. **This is the margin SKU.**
- *Positioning:* against ₹3,000–5,000 professional local shifting, ₹1,299 with two porters
  is not a discount — it is a different product.

**8. MB-FLY-01 · Flight Day Rescue — ₹1,199 for 20 kg**
Same-day pickup from hostel or PG on the morning of the flight; the student flies with only
their 15 kg allowance and we ship the rest.
- *Relatability:* solves the panic scenario, not the planning scenario. ₹1,199 against
  ₹12,000 at the counter.
- *Volume:* MEDIUM–HIGH, clustered tightly around break dates.

**9. MB-PASS-03 · Box Bank Pass — ₹2,499 for 3 shipments, valid the academic year**
₹833 per box instead of ₹999.
- *Relatability:* the parent-facing SKU. "One payment, stop asking Dad for courier money."
- *Volume:* LOW count, HIGH cash. This is the **cash-flow** product: money now, service later,
  and unredeemed boxes are real margin. Print an expiry date and terms on the card.

### TIER 3 — Strategic, not volume

**10. MB-AMB-01 · Campus Ambassador Programme — free** *(growth engine)*
Sign 20–30 ambassadors at the stall. ₹100 per box shipped, ₹300 per PG shift, ₹150 per
storage booking, paid by UPI against a personal code.
- *The CHRIST-specific unlock:* offer a signed **"Campus Growth Intern — MoveABox"
  certificate, LOR-eligible**. CHRIST students actively collect internship credentials for
  their CVs and portfolios. At a *startup fair*, that offer will fill the sign-up sheet on
  its own — and it costs us nothing.
- *30-day lift:* trivial. A form, a code each, a WhatsApp group.

**11. MB-CLUB-01 · Fest & Club Logistics Retainer — ₹4,999 per semester**
Three vehicle-days plus prop storage between events. CHRIST runs Darpan, Blossoms, InBloom,
Magnovite and dozens of departmental fests; clubs move sets, sound gear, backdrops and
sponsor stock constantly, and currently do it with borrowed cars.
- *Relatability:* club office-bearers are exactly the people staffing a startup fair. Sign
  MOUs across the table.
- *Volume:* LOW count, HIGH ticket — and it buys standing permission to be on campus
  repeatedly, which is worth more than the fee.

**12. MB-BUY-01 · Semester-End Haul-Away — MoveABox credit, not cash** *(pilot)*
Mattress, table fan, induction cooktop, cycle, study lamp — we take it away, they get credit
against a future shipment. We resell to incoming juniors in June.
- *Relatability:* very high. The alternative is dumping it or an OLX chat that never converts.
- *Scope it as credit-only in month one.* Cash-out exposure is then zero, and the credit
  locks them into a future booking. Cash offers can come in v2 once resale is proven.
- *Volume:* MEDIUM. Strategic value is the second revenue line — used goods sold to freshers
  in June, at near-zero cost of goods.

**13. MB-CYCLE-01 · Cycle & Two-Wheeler Transport — lead capture only**
Genuinely unsolved, emotionally significant (it's their Activa), and ₹2,999–4,999 a unit —
but it needs crating and a bike-carrier partner. **Do not sell this at the stall.** Collect
names and quote later; over-promising here is the fastest way to a bad first review.

## 6. The stall itself

Four mechanics, in order of expected impact:

1. **A weighing scale on the table.** "Put your bag on it." It dramatises the 15 kg wall
   physically, in two seconds, without anyone reading a word. Best single mechanic available
   to a mover at a campus fair.
2. **A wall of stacked branded boxes as the backdrop.** MoveABox literally is the box — make
   the stall look like moving day rather than like a bank counter.
3. **"Where's home?" — an India map with pins.** Students pin their home city. It is a photo
   moment, it makes a logistics company feel personal, and it is genuine data collection:
   by the end of the fair we know our lane density and can price pooling properly.
4. **Live rate calculator on a tablet.** Type a pincode, see the price. The API is already
   wired into the ERP — this is a two-hour build and it makes the pricing feel honest.

## 7. 30-day build plan

**Week 1 (18–24 Aug)** — Confirm the stall in writing with the Incubation & Entrepreneurship
Support Cell / Office of Alumni Engagement, including permission to sell and collect payment
on campus. Lock the SKU list and pricing. Place the carton order (600 units, 5–7 day lead).

**Week 2 (25–31 Aug)** — Build the voucher system: unique codes, redemption sheet, WhatsApp
confirmation flow. One UPI QR per SKU so reconciliation is automatic. Set the Delhivery zone
matrix behind the flat price. Print banner, rate card, standee.

**Week 3 (1–7 Sep)** — Staff: two from Koramangala branch plus four student ambassadors
recruited early. Rehearse the pitch. Build the tablet rate calculator. Prepare the scale demo.

**Week 4 (8–18 Sep)** — Soft launch: two live test shipments, one live storage pickup, and
20 pre-registered leads seeded through CHRIST WhatsApp groups. Stock the stall. Go.

## 8. Risks and things to verify

- **Academic calendar.** The whole voucher-expiry and capacity plan hinges on the exact
  odd-semester exam and vacate dates. The CHRIST site was unreachable from this environment
  — **confirm the 2026-27 calendar directly before printing any expiry date on a voucher.**
  Working assumption: the demand wave lands late Oct–Nov 2026 (Dasara/Diwali break plus
  odd-semester exams), which is precisely why a September stall pre-selling into November
  is well-timed.
- **Fair details unconfirmed.** Name, date, footfall and stall fee were not verifiable
  publicly. CHRIST's known entrepreneurship events are Start'o'Sphere (the annual flagship
  startup conclave) and Mercaton (the campus marketplace) — worth confirming which this is,
  because Mercaton implies a selling audience and Start'o'Sphere implies an investor/founder
  audience, and the SKU mix should tilt accordingly.
- **Campus selling permission.** Get it in writing. Some campuses restrict on-site payment
  collection. Issue GST invoices on every sale regardless — students won't care, the
  university will.
- **Capacity during exam week.** Do not promise same-day fulfilment in the vacate window.
  Publish a pickup-slot calendar and cap slots per day.
- **Voucher disputes.** Print expiry, coverage and exclusions on the back of every card.
  Ambiguity here surfaces as one-star reviews in April.

## 9. Bottom line

The three that will do the most work: **the ₹349 Vacate Kit** (volume and visibility), **the
₹999 Campus-to-Home Box** (the pitch, backed by a 13x price advantage that is genuinely
true), and **the ₹399 Parcel Desk** (the only SKU that makes MoveABox a weekly habit rather
than an annual one).

The one to build the campaign around: **Group Box pooling plus the ambassador certificate.**
That combination is what turns a one-day stall into recurring campus volume — everything
else is a single transaction.
