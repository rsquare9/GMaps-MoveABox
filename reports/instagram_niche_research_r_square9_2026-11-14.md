## 2. The niche landscape

| Adjacent niche | Saturation | Who owns it | Your edge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal finance / investing | Very high | Warikoo, Sharan Hegde, Rachana Ranade, Pranjal Kamra | Low, crowded with big names |
| Stock trading / intraday | Very high + **regulated** | Countless finfluencers | Risky. SEBI bars unregistered creators from buy/sell calls; educational price data must be 3+ months old; new disclosure rules since May 2026 |
| MBA admissions | Medium | MBA Crystal Ball, @mba_secrets, @mbasocial (~192K), meme pages | Real-time *applicant* POV is rare; most accounts are consultants or alumni |
| Startup founder content | High (generic) | Founder-creators, mostly tech/SaaS/D2C | **Very few show an unglamorous offline business** with real ops |
| Bangalore logistics / moving business | Almost empty | No one | You own this by default |

**Key insight:** "Finance guy gives advice" is crowded. **"Finance guy who actually runs a trucks-and-boxes business in Bangalore, and is applying to a top MBA"** is a niche of one.

## 3. Recommended niche

### Primary positioning
> **"Corporate finance by training, logistics founder by choice. Building MoveABox in Bangalore and documenting the real numbers."**

Short bio version (≤150 chars):
> Founder @moveabox 📦 | Ex-KPMG risk · JPMC | Economics @ Christ | Building a Bangalore logistics business in public | MBA '27 aspirant

*(Adjust the MBA line to your actual target intake; drop employers you'd rather not name publicly.)*

### Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | Why it works | Example reels |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Building MoveABox in public** | 40% | Unique, unglamorous, real. "Boring business" content performs well and nobody does it in Bangalore logistics | "What a ₹15,000 house shift costs me to deliver" · "Our worst moving day" · "Hiring packers vs hiring analysts" · monthly revenue/learnings |
| **2. Finance brain × small business** | 25% | Uses your training without giving regulated advice | "How a risk analyst prices a moving job" · unit economics of a truck · cash flow lessons from running a startup alongside a job · GST/invoice mistakes |
| **3. The MBA journey (live)** | 20% | Applicant-side, real-time content is scarce; peaks during R1/R2 seasons | GMAT/GRE progress · essay angles from a founder profile · school visits/coffee chats · results-day reaction (win or lose) |
| **4. Career & Bangalore life** | 15% | Relatability and reach | Job + startup schedule · lessons from KPMG/JPMC · Bangalore founder life |

**Trading:** keep it off the main grid or limit it to *process and psychology* (journaling, risk sizing, mistakes). No stock calls, no live trade tips. It's the most crowded niche, under SEBI scrutiny, and it dilutes the founder story.

## 4. Account hygiene (do first)

1. **Rewrite bio** (above) and add a link hub: MoveABox site, LinkedIn, optional newsletter.
2. **Fix the follow ratio.** Gradually unfollow inactive or irrelevant accounts until following is below followers. Target <1,500 following.
3. **Archive off-niche posts.** Keep the top 9 grid on-message. Archive, don't delete, old personal posts.
4. **Pin 3 reels:** (a) who you are and why MoveABox, (b) your best business-numbers reel, (c) MBA journey kickoff.
5. **Switch to a Creator account** if not already, so you get Insights and topic labels.
6. **Story highlights:** MoveABox · Numbers · MBA · About.

---

## 5. Growth playbook

- **Cadence:** 3 reels/week + stories most days. One weekly "numbers" reel becomes the signature series (e.g. *"MoveABox Monday: this week's numbers"*).
- **Format:** Talking-head + B-roll from real job sites (trucks, packing, warehouse). Captions always. Hook in 2 seconds with a number or a contradiction ("I left a finance job to move furniture").
- **Cross-post:** The same scripts work as LinkedIn posts. LinkedIn is where your finance and MBA audience lives and it compounds with Instagram.
- **Synergy with @moveabox:** The founder account builds trust and the brand account converts. Collab-post (Instagram Collab feature) the best MoveABox reels so they appear on both grids.
- **MBA tie-in:** A documented founder story with an audience is a strong signal for MBA essays and interviews. Treat the account as part of your application narrative.
- **Collabs:** Bangalore founder creators, Christ University alumni, MBA applicant communities, ops/logistics creators.
